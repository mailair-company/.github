**Mailair** gives small teams the connected tools big companies get from enterprise suites, without the price tag or the IT department.

- **Email marketing** — campaigns, audiences, follow-up sequences, and open/click tracking
- **One inbox, sorted by AI** — Gmail and Outlook together, with urgent emails first, action items pulled out, and replies drafted in your voice
- **Team workspace** — shared docs, files, chat, and built-in voice and video calls
- **Fits your stack** — HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and Google Calendar, plus iOS and Android apps

Built with Next.js, FastAPI, Supabase. Source: [mailair-company/mailair](https://github.com/mailair-company/mailair).
